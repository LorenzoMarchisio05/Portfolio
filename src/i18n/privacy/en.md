## Who is responsible

Lorenzo Marchisio, reachable at [me@lorenzomarchisio.me](mailto:me@lorenzomarchisio.me).

## What is collected

**Contact form** (on the home page): your name, email and message. The form also sends the language of the page you were reading, so I can reply in it.

## Why, and on what legal basis

To read and reply to your message. The legal basis is my legitimate interest in replying to messages sent to me (GDPR, Article 6(1)(f)). Giving me your details is voluntary; without them I cannot reply. No automated decisions or profiling take place.

## How long it is kept

Messages are kept for 12 months if no project starts, then deleted. If a project starts, they are kept for as long as the contract and accounting law require.

## Who receives it

Messages sent through the form are delivered to me by email only; nothing is stored in a database. These processors handle data on my behalf: **Resend** (email delivery), my email provider, and **Cloudflare** (hosting and delivery of this site). Resend and Cloudflare are based in the United States; those transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. Your data is never sold or shared for advertising.

## Server logs

Like any website, this one is served through Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and basic request data to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse. The contact form also keeps your IP address in memory for a few minutes to limit repeated submissions; it is not written anywhere. The basis is my legitimate interest in running a secure site (Article 6(1)(f)).

## Photographs

The photo gallery shows photographs I took. If you appear in one and want it removed, use the removal link in the gallery or email me, and I will take it down promptly.

## Cookies and storage

This site sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

## Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format. Email [me@lorenzomarchisio.me](mailto:me@lorenzomarchisio.me). You can also complain to a data protection authority, such as the Belgian Data Protection Authority or the one where you live (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).
